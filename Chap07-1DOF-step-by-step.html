4 · Free vibration by each scheme Paz 6.5, 16.2

y/y₀ against t/T; grey: exact. Below Δt/T = 0.551 linear acceleration is the most accurate; just above (try 0.56) it grows ×1.23 per step. Average acceleration never grows, but its period lengthens. Constant ÿᵢ grows at every Δt when ξ = 0. Wilson with θ ≥ 1.37 never grows and damps the motion; Paz's form (16.24) grows for every θ > 1 (try Δt/T = 0.1, 50 periods).
