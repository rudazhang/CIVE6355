Every mode at once Paz 7.2

n floors, n modes. In one mode all floors pass through zero together and stop together. Mode j has j − 1 sign changes, and each one puts more drift into the stories for the same floor motion: more strain energy over the same mass term, so a higher frequency. Open circles: nodes. Shading: each story's share of the strain energy. Sweep n on the uniform building: T₁ grows about in proportion to n, the top frequency stops growing. Tap a building to enlarge it.

Drag a trial shape Paz 7.4; 2024 notes L22

Every trial shape lands inside [ω₁², ωₙ²]: R(y) is where a beam balances with a weight at each ωⱼ², and a balance point cannot leave its weights. A shape with sign changes jumps up the axis. Sensible shapes land just above ω₁², so R(y) gives a quick upper bound on the fundamental, and how good depends on the shape. Beside each floor: the ω² that floor would need. A mode is the shape on which all floors agree. R(y) is Rayleigh's quotient, not the flexibility matrix [R] of deck 08.

Rayleigh's quotient on the unit sphere the slide's polar plot, one dimension up

Every direction {y} reaches out to √R(y): the surface comes closest along mode 1, reaches farthest along mode 3, and has a saddle at mode 2, where two curves of R = ω₂² cross. Each trial shape is a point on this surface, so R(y) cannot leave [ω₁², ω₃²]. Near mode 1 the surface is flat: a small error in the shape barely changes R. Click or tap the surface to put the trial shape there; drag to turn the view; on a touch screen pinch to zoom, and move or twist two fingers to pan or roll. The red dots are the trial shape {y₀} and −{y₀} (R is the same); step the iteration and a path follows the method shown in the panel below, in that method's color (red: Rayleigh quotient iteration), each {yk} at its own √R.

Rayleigh quotient iteration not in Paz; 2024 notes L22, L29; Chopra Ex. 10.14–10.16

Digits roughly triple each step, and it finds whichever mode the start favors, not the lowest: the count of sign changes tells which mode came out. The table defines the methods: tick the ones to plot, pick the one to show in the building, the table of iterates and on the sphere.

Hold for p, first shift r₀ set the shifted start: the shift stays at r₀ for the cycles k = 0, …, p (p = 0: once). That is inverse iteration with a fixed shift: it pulls toward the ωⱼ² nearest r₀, at a fixed factor per step. Then the shift follows rk and the digits triple. Empty r₀: R(y₀).
